## 2. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Family Safety)

Teach Your Kiddos is designed for children (ages 3 to 12) under the direct supervision and guidance of a parent, legal guardian, or educator.

- **Parental Account Ownership:** Accounts are created, authenticated, and managed exclusively by parents or adult legal guardians.
- **No Direct Child Data Harvesting:** We do not knowingly collect, require, or solicit personal contact information (such as email addresses, telephone numbers, or physical locations) directly from children.
- **Child Profile Information:** Parents may enter an optional first name or nickname and age to calibrate the educational AI reading level and math difficulty. This information is stored securely and solely used to tailor educational content to the child's developmental age.
- **Zero Third-Party Advertising:** Teach Your Kiddos contains no behavioral advertising, no third-party ad tracking SDKs, and no sponsored commercial content.
- **Parental Rights:** Parents and legal guardians have the right at any time to review, modify, or permanently delete their child's profile information or saved stories by contacting us at [hemanthnarayanan123@gmail.com](mailto:hemanthnarayanan123@gmail.com) or using the in-app settings.

## 3. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
1. **Parent Account Information:** When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive basic authentication tokens, your name, email address, and profile picture to create and manage your secure account.
2. **Child Educational Settings:** Child nickname, age level (3–12), voice narration preferences, and reading speed.
3. **User-Generated Stories & Learning Progress:** Story generation prompts, saved storybooks, chapter reading progress, quiz scores, and math quest achievements.

### B. Google User Data & Google Drive Integration
Teach Your Kiddos allows parents to optionally connect their Google Account to back up and synchronize personalized storybooks with their personal cloud storage.

When you grant permission, our application accesses:
- **Google Profile & Email:** To authenticate your parent account securely via Firebase Authentication.
- **Google Drive (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file`):** This is a restricted, least-privilege permission. Teach Your Kiddos **only** accesses, creates, modifies, and deletes files that were directly created by or opened with Teach Your Kiddos (specifically, your saved storybook JSON files stored in your Drive). Teach Your Kiddos **cannot** see, browse, access, or modify any other files, photos, or documents in your Google Drive.

## 5. How We Use Collected Information

We use the collected information solely for legitimate educational and service delivery purposes:
- To generate age-appropriate reading stories and math challenges tailored to your child's age group.
- To maintain your child's library and sync saved storybooks to your Google Drive when requested.

## 6. How Your Information is Stored and Protected

- **Cloud & Local Storage:** Educational storybooks are cached locally in your browser's IndexedDB/sessionStorage for instant offline access, and synchronized to your personal Google Drive account when enabled.
- **Data Security Measures:** All data transmissions between your browser and our servers or Google APIs use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3) encryption.
- **Access Tokens:** Google OAuth access tokens are stored securely in temporary session storage and are never logged, exposed to third parties, or saved on insecure external disks.
